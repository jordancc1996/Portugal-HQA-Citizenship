Site: portugalhqacitizenship.com
Primary keyword: portugal hqa visa investment
Program: the "HQA" investment route to Portuguese residency (incubator and university R&D packages, often priced at about €175,000)
Audience: HNWIs, founders and families comparing Golden Visa alternatives
Conversion: email leads through Formcarry
Build: Astro, built in Cursor, deployed on Vercel
Research date: October 19, 2026

---

## 1. Findings that shape the whole site

1. **"HQA visa" is a marketing label, not a law.** The legal basis is the D3 residence visa (art. 61) and the residence permit for highly qualified activity (art. 90) in Lei 23/2007. Portuguese law sets **no minimum investment** for this route. The €175,000 figure is the price of private programmes (incubator, university R&D work, company setup, legal fees). The only statutory investment route is the Golden Visa (art. 90-A).
2. **Citizenship now takes 10 years.** Lei Orgânica n.º 1/2026 was published on May 18, 2026 and has been in force since May 19, 2026. It requires 10 years of legal residence (7 for EU and CPLP nationals). Applications pending on May 19, 2026 keep the old rules. Verified in the official text (Diário da República n.º 95/2026).
3. **Almost every competitor is out of date or unclear.** Most pages about the €175K product still say "citizenship in 5 years", repeat "no minimum stay" without evidence, ignore IFICI, cite SEF (abolished in 2023), and mix up the employment D3 route with the investment package. None cites the new nationality law.
4. **Trust is the main buyer worry.** Forum users ask whether HQA is legitimate, what the legal basis is, whether anyone has done it, whether the money is refundable, and what happens if the startup fails.
5. **Strategic position:** be the honest, sourced, up-to-date reference on the HQA investment route. Honesty about the 10-year timeline and the risks is the selling point, not a weakness.

### Two risks to check before launch

- **Trademark:** Empowered Startups markets "HQA®" and "HQA Visa™". Have a lawyer check use of "HQA" in the domain, brand and copy.
- **Domain wording:** the domain promises "citizenship". Content must frame citizenship as a long-term (10-year) path and never promise it.

### Questions for a Portuguese lawyer before launch

1. Which legal article do the €175K programmes file under: art. 90(1)(a), 90(1)(c), 61-A or 89(4)? This decides family and renewal rules.
2. Can a founder qualify through an employment contract with a company they own? What salary must they draw?
3. Current euro salary thresholds for art. 61-A and the Tech Visa (how "2.5 × IAS" is read).
4. When exactly does the citizenship clock start under LO 1/2026, and does D3 visa time or AIMA waiting time count?
5. Do the new language and civics duties (art. 101) apply to families of art. 90 holders?
6. Current AIMA fees and consular fee.
7. What happens at renewal if the startup fails or loses certification?
8. Are the claimed university partnerships and provider "government authority" claims genuine?
9. Trademark exposure for "HQA" in the domain and brand.

## 3. Search intent

The top results for "portugal hqa visa investment" are long editorial guides (Portugalist, IMI Daily, The Portugal News). The searcher already knows the €175K figure and wants to check it: is it legitimate, what does the money buy, how much time must I spend in Portugal, how long until citizenship now, and is it better than the €500K Golden Visa fund route?

Winning format: a dated, sourced guide of 2,500+ words with a key facts block, cost table, timeline graphic, comparison table, FAQs and a soft lead magnet.

## 5. Content gaps we can own

1. An honest citizenship timeline under the 10-year law (year 0 to year 10, with the permanent residence option at year 5).
2. Presence rules explained properly: keeping the permit vs renewing it vs counting time for citizenship.
3. A true all-in cost breakdown: programme price, dependents, government fees, translations, renewals, what you own afterwards, refund terms.
4. One clear page on what "HQA" actually means (employment D3 vs investment package).
5. A due-diligence checklist for providers and incubators (also the lead magnet).
6. IFICI explained for HQA founders, including US tax interaction.
7. A dated status tracker with a changelog.
8. Primary-law citations on every page, which no competitor does.
