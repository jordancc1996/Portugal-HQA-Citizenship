## 1. Route description, package price, marketing claims

- Article 90 does not require a €175,000 investment. It requires the article 77 conditions and one of the statutory activity/documentation pathways. A package does not make the price a statutory qualification or guarantee eligibility.
- Preferred wording: "Article 90 does not set a minimum investment payment. Some private providers sell packages that include company and research-project services; their price and scope are provider-specific."
- Every package figure (€175,000 total, €150,000/€25,000 split, 3-year incubation, timing, fees) is a provider-market fact: attribute, date and link each one, and keep dated source evidence.
- Avoid "real" as an unverified quality judgment. "It is not a deposit" and "returns are not promised" need the actual contract terms; a categorical statement could mislead if a payment is refundable, escrowed or repayable.
- A future sale of leads is a material financial relationship: disclose it clearly beside the introduction and before the user submits data.

## 2. Family reunification

- Article 98 (Lei 61/2025): 2-year wait; 15 months for a spouse/partner who cohabited at least 18 months before entry. No wait for dependent minors or incapacitated members, a spouse/partner who is parent/adoptive parent of a dependent minor or incapacitated person, and families of articles 90, 90-A and 121-A holders. Other exceptional cases may be dispensed or reduced by ministerial order. "Most article 89 families wait" needs those qualifications. Valid recognised marriage or union, both at least 18 at the application date.
- Article 105: 9-month decision period; one equal extension for exceptional complexity, with notice, except for the article 98(1) and (2) cases. Article 90 families appear eligible for the extension on the enacted text. State it is available only when the exceptional-complexity test is met, not automatic, and never present "up to 18 months" as routine. AIMA practice is unconfirmed.
- Article 101(3)-(5): integration duties apply to family members generally, including article 90 families. Details depend on a regulatory decree not supplied.
- Family-permit duration ("same length as the main applicant's permit"): not confirmed. Check article 107 before keeping it.
- Lei 61/2025 transitional rules: amendments apply to procedures started after entry into force; filing date and status can change which regime applies. Date the rules.

## 3. Citizenship

- Lei Orgânica 1/2026: 7 years for nationals of Portuguese-official-language countries and EU citizens, 10 years for others.
- Add the article 6(10) presumption with its limit: "For nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language, the law presumes the Portuguese-language element is met unless lack of Portuguese is manifest. That presumption does not remove the separate requirements concerning Portuguese culture, history, and national symbols."
- Use "naturalisation" or "application for Portuguese nationality", not "passport". Note article 6 has cumulative conditions beyond residence and language.
- Criminal bar: final conviction with an effective prison sentence greater than three years for the listed crimes. The revised sentence is more accurate.
- Do not rely on Acórdão 1133/2025 as support for any current rule.
- Present 7 and 10 years as minimum residence conditions, not guarantees. Confirm transitional applicability.

## 4. Absence limits and Golden Visa comparison

- Article 85(2): a temporary permit MAY be cancelled (discretionary) after 6 consecutive or 8 interpolated months outside Portugal during the permit's total validity, absent acceptable reasons. 85(3): justify a longer absence to AIMA before departure, exceptionally after. 85(4): not cancelled if the holder proves professional, business, cultural or social activity during the absence.
- Permanent residence: 24 consecutive months or 30 interpolated months in 3 years. Do not let temporary limits appear to apply to permanent status.
- "Live in Portugal" and "no minimum stay" are imprecise; define by the law and permit type.
- Renewal row: state initial validity and renewal separately. HQA: 2 years, then 3-year renewals (article 75). Golden Visa: 2-year renewals (article 90-A(2)).
- Low-density reduction: verify the article 3(2)-(3) text, the low-density definition and conditions before keeping it.

## 5. Permanent residence

Preferred: "Permanent residence status has no fixed expiry date. The residence card must be renewed every five years, and sooner if a registered identification detail changes." Cite article 76(1)-(2) and article 80 accurately.

## 6. IFICI and Tech Visa

- IFICI: the scope limit to the highly qualified professions category in Portaria 352/2024/1 is sound; verify category by category.
- Tech Visa: track the official IAPMEI wording (ISCED 2011 level 6, or level 5 with five years' experience in specialised technical functions) and check the Portuguese text of the portaria. Education is not the only condition.

## 7. Privacy: controller identity (launch blocker)

The brand name alone does not identify the GDPR controller. Article 13(1)(a) needs the real natural or legal person and contact details. Check Decreto-Lei 7/2004 (e-commerce) and consumer rules for operator identification. Do not describe Formcarry or Vercel as having "no own purposes" without reviewing their terms and DPAs; an Article 28 processor contract is needed if they act on instructions.

## 8. Purposes, checklist, retention, cookies, form data

- The checklist purpose is wrong: the site shows a link to a public PDF; it does not email it. Reconsider whether a personal-data gate is needed for a public file at all.
- Retention proposals (adapt and state plainly): checklist records deleted within about 30 days unless the person opted into updates; consultation requests kept only while arranging the introduction, then deleted or anonymised after about 6 months; update emails kept until unsubscribe, with an inactivity review such as 24 months; minimal suppression record only.
- Check the deployed site for cookies and third-party requests before stating "no cookies".
- Message box: tell users not to submit passports, medical details, criminal records or children's information. Avoid soliciting Article 9 or Article 10 data.

## 9. Selling leads

Do not sell or disclose leads under the current notice and consent. A lead-sale model needs: identified controller, controller/joint-controller analysis, named recipients or specific categories, a documented legal basis, Article 13 information at collection, a separate unticked opt-in not bundled with the introduction request, data minimisation, Article 14 notices from recipients, and prominent disclosure of any referral fee or payment at the decision point.

## 10. Site operator, terms, page-wide risks

Online-service identification rules may apply. Check /about, /contact, /consultation and /terms for operator identity, contact details, the nature of the introduction service, whether the site is paid, and the line between information and individual advice. Define what the consultation promises: whether the site screens advisors or checks licences, forwards a message, receives value. If no screening is done, say so.

## 11. Triage

Ready in principle (subject to full-page check): provider price attribution; removing "most investors"; limiting company/research claims; removing unsourced backlog claims; "legal claims cite official sources where available"; no legal minimum payment; article 90 invitation wording; CPLP language-only presumption; both absence thresholds with exceptions; permanent status vs card; checklist purpose matching form behaviour.

Correct or qualify before publication: €175,000 and breakdown as dated provider claims; "may be cancelled"; 2-year initial plus 3-year renewals; article 105 extension as exceptional; article 101 regulatory dependency; controller identity; category-specific retention; live cookie check; no lead sale under current consent.

Not approvable from the packet: full page claims, privacy and terms; provider prices/fees/refund statements; tax claims, IAS and FEIE; article 80 conditions; family-permit duration; low-density thresholds; Tech Visa wording; Constitutional Court support.
