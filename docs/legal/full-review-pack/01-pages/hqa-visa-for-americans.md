# Portugal HQA Visa for Americans: What US Investors Should Know

**P1.** The Portugal HQA visa for Americans works the same way as for any other non-EU applicant, with one big difference: the United States taxes its citizens wherever they live. US citizens can apply for the highly qualified activity route, move to Portugal and, after 10 years of legal residence, apply for Portuguese citizenship. You will still file US tax returns every year.

## Key facts

**P4.** Same Portuguese rules. Americans follow the same visa, permit and residence rules as other non-EU nationals.

**P5.** 10 years to citizenship. The US is not an EU country, and Portuguese is not an official language there, so the 10-year rule applies.

**P6.** US tax does not stop. US citizens must file US tax returns while living abroad.

**P7.** Dual citizenship is possible. Both the US and Portugal allow it.

**P8.** The 10-year rule for US citizens is explained in the citizenship guide.

## How Americans apply

**P9.** Choose a provider and sign the package contract, with your own Portuguese lawyer reviewing it.

**P10.** Get a Portuguese tax number (NIF) and open a Portuguese bank account, usually with your lawyer's help.

**P11.** Gather your documents: passport, degree certificates, FBI background check, proof of funds and accommodation. US documents usually need an apostille.

**P12.** Apply for the D3 visa through the Portuguese consulate that covers your US state.

**P13.** Travel to Portugal and attend your AIMA appointment for the residence permit.

## Can I keep my US citizenship?

**P20.** Yes. The United States does not require you to give up your citizenship when you become Portuguese, and Portugal allows dual citizenship. You can hold both nationalities.

## Living in Portugal from the US

**P21.** Absence limits apply. A temporary permit may be cancelled after more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 non-consecutive months, outside Portugal during the permit's total validity, unless the absence is justified.

## Frequently asked questions

**P32.** Can Americans apply for the Portugal HQA visa?

**P33.** Yes. US citizens follow the same rules as other non-EU applicants.

**P34.** How long until an American can get Portuguese citizenship?

**P35.** 10 years of legal residence under the law in force since 19 May 2026.

**P36.** Do I still pay US taxes if I live in Portugal?

**P37.** You still file US returns. Whether you owe US tax depends on your income, exclusions, credits and the US–Portugal tax treaty.

**P38.** Where do Americans apply for the visa?

**P39.** At the Portuguese consulate that covers your US state of residence.

**P40.** Do I need an FBI background check?

**P41.** Yes. Portugal asks for criminal record certificates, and for Americans that usually means an FBI check, often apostilled.
