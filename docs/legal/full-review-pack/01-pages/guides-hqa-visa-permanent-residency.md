# HQA Visa Permanent Residency: What Changes After 5 Years

**P1.** HQA visa permanent residency becomes possible after 5 years of temporary residence in Portugal. A permanent residence permit means you stop renewing a temporary permit every few years. It does not give you Portuguese nationality, and it does not shorten the 10-year wait for citizenship.

**P2.** Last verified: 20 October 2026

**P3.** This page is general information, not legal advice.

## Key facts

**P4.** After 5 years. You need at least 5 years with a temporary residence permit.

**P5.** Basic Portuguese. You must prove basic knowledge of the language.

**P6.** No fixed expiry date. Permanent residence status has no fixed expiry date. The residence card must be renewed every five years, and sooner if a registered identification detail changes (article 76).

**P7.** Not citizenship. Most people still need 10 years of legal residence before they can apply for Portuguese nationality.

**P8.** Source: Lei 23/2007, articles 76 and 80.

## Why HQA holders may want permanent residence

**P12.** You no longer depend on the company. A temporary HQA permit depends on your highly qualified activity. Permanent residence does not.

**P13.** Fewer renewals. One card every 5 years.

**P14.** A bridge to citizenship. It keeps you legally resident while you build up to 10 years.

### What we don't know yet

**P15.** Whether the new citizenship rules will change how years on a permanent residence card are counted. The law counts legal residence under any valid permit, and we expect permanent residence to count. We will confirm this when the government publishes its implementing rules.

## Frequently asked questions

**P23.** When can I get permanent residency on the HQA route?

**P24.** After at least 5 years with a temporary residence permit, if you meet the other requirements.

**P25.** What level of Portuguese do I need?

**P26.** Basic knowledge, proved with a recognised certificate, such as a CAPLE test or an A2-level Portuguese course.

**P27.** Does permanent residence give me Portuguese nationality?

**P28.** No. Nationality is a separate application. Most people can apply after 10 years of legal residence (7 for EU nationals and nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language), and residence is only one of the conditions.

**P29.** Do I still need my HQA company after I get permanent residence?

**P30.** Your permanent residence does not depend on the highly qualified activity. Your investment and company are a separate matter.

**P31.** How long is the permanent residence card valid?

**P32.** Permanent residence status has no fixed expiry date. The residence card must be renewed every five years, and sooner if a registered identification detail changes (article 76).
