# HQA Visa Citizenship: The 10-Year Rule Explained

**P1.** HQA visa citizenship now takes 10 years of legal residence for most applicants. Portugal changed its nationality law with Lei Orgânica n.º 1/2026, which has been in force since 19 May 2026. EU nationals and nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language need 7 years. The 7 years and the 10 years are minimum residence conditions, not guarantees. Many websites still promise Portuguese nationality after 5 years. That is no longer true for new applications.

## Key facts

**P4.** 10 years for most people. Applicants from outside the EU, and from outside countries where Portuguese is an official language, need 10 years of legal residence.

**P5.** 7 years for EU nationals and nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language. EU nationals and nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language need 7 years.

**P6.** In force since 19 May 2026. The law was published on 18 May 2026.

**P7.** Pending applications keep the old rules. Applications already pending on 19 May 2026 are decided under the previous law.

**P8.** Source: Lei Orgânica n.º 1/2026, Diário da República n.º 95/2026.

## What we don't know yet

**P14.** When the clock starts. The old rule that counted time from your permit application was revoked. Several law firms say the clock now starts when your first residence card is issued. The law text does not say this directly. It defines legal residence as living in Portugal with your situation regularised under any visa or permit. Whether time on your D3 visa, or time spent waiting for AIMA, counts is not settled.

**P15.** The implementing rules. The government must update the nationality regulation to apply the new law. Until it does, details such as the language level and test format are not final.

## The other requirements for citizenship

**P17.** Residence is one of several conditions in article 6, alongside age, language and culture, means of subsistence, security checks and the criminal-record test.

**P18.** Language. You must show knowledge of Portuguese through a test or certificate. The required level is set by regulation, not by the law itself. For nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language, the law presumes the Portuguese-language element is met unless lack of Portuguese is manifest. That presumption does not remove the separate requirements concerning Portuguese culture, history, and national symbols.

**P19.** Knowledge of rights and duties. A test of civic knowledge is part of the new law.

**P20.** Means of support. You must show you can support yourself.

**P21.** Criminal record. A final conviction with an effective prison sentence of more than 3 years for certain serious crimes can block an application.

## How the law got here

**P22.** 18 May 2026. Parliament's revised law was published as Lei Orgânica n.º 1/2026.

**P23.** 19 May 2026. The law came into force.

## Does the Golden Visa get you citizenship faster?

**P24.** No. Golden Visa holders follow the same 10-year rule (7 years for EU nationals and nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language). The Golden Visa asks for less time in Portugal each year, but it does not shorten the citizenship wait.

## Frequently asked questions

**P33.** How long does HQA visa citizenship take now?

**P34.** Most applicants need 10 years of legal residence. EU nationals and nationals of countries where Portuguese is an official language need 7 years.

**P35.** I already applied for citizenship before 19 May 2026. Does the new rule apply to me?

**P36.** No. Applications already pending on 19 May 2026 are decided under the previous rules.

**P37.** I had 5 years of residence on 19 May 2026 but had not applied. Am I protected?

**P38.** No. The transition rule covers pending applications only. If you had not applied by that date, the new rules apply to you.

**P39.** Can I keep my current citizenship?

**P40.** Portugal allows dual citizenship. Whether you can keep your current citizenship depends on the law of your own country.

**P41.** Is permanent residence the same as citizenship?

**P42.** No. Permanent residence is possible after 5 years and lets you stop renewing a temporary permit. Portuguese nationality needs 10 years of legal residence for most people, and residence is only one of the conditions.
