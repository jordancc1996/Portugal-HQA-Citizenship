# HQA vs StartUP Visa Portugal: And the Tech Visa

**P1.** HQA vs Startup Visa Portugal is a common comparison because the routes look alike: both involve a young company and an incubator. The difference is in the legal basis. HQA packages aim for a residence permit for highly qualified activity under article 90. The StartUP Visa uses article 89, which has different family rules. The Tech Visa is a third route, for people hired by a certified company.

## Key facts

**P4.** Different articles. HQA uses article 90, the StartUP Visa uses article 89, the Tech Visa uses article 90 through a certified company.

**P5.** Family rules differ. Article 90 families are exempt from the 2-year wait. Article 89 families are not in that list, so the wait applies unless one of the exceptions does.

**P6.** Different costs. HQA packages cost about €175,000, reported by providers, October 2026. No government body sets or checks that price. The StartUP Visa has no investment minimum.

**P7.** All lead to the same citizenship rule. 10 years of legal residence for most nationals.

**P8.** Article 90 families and the 2-year wait are explained on the family page. The package price is explained on the cost page. The shared citizenship rule is explained in the citizenship guide.

## Frequently asked questions

**P27.** What is the difference between the HQA visa and the StartUP Visa?

**P28.** The HQA route uses article 90 (highly qualified activity). The StartUP Visa uses article 89 and needs acceptance by an IAPMEI-certified incubator. The family rules differ.

**P29.** Is the StartUP Visa cheaper?

**P30.** There is no investment minimum, but you build and fund your own startup, and incubators may charge fees.

**P31.** What is the Tech Visa?

**P32.** A route for skilled workers hired by a company certified for the Tech Visa by IAPMEI. It does not need an investment.

**P33.** Which route gets citizenship fastest?

**P34.** None. All three need 10 years of legal residence for most nationals.

**P35.** Can I switch from the StartUP Visa to an HQA permit later?

**P36.** Possibly, if you later meet the highly qualified activity conditions. Ask a Portuguese lawyer, because a change of permit type has its own requirements.
