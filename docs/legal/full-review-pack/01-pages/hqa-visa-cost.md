# HQA Visa Portugal Cost: The Full Breakdown

**P1.** HQA visa Portugal cost starts with a private package of about €175,000, reported by providers, October 2026, but it does not end there. No government body sets or checks that price. You will also pay for family members, legal work, government fees, documents and renewals. This page lists every cost we know of, who sets it, and how sure we are of the number. Where we could not confirm a figure, we say so.

## Key facts

**P4.** No minimum payment in article 90. Article 90 does not set a minimum investment payment. Some private providers sell packages that include company and research-project services; their price and scope are provider-specific.

**P5.** Packages cost about €170,000 to €200,000. €175,000 is the most common quote, reported by providers, October 2026. No government body sets or checks them.

**P6.** Family members cost extra. Providers quote about €2,500 to €5,000 per dependent.

**P7.** Government fees changed on 1 March 2026. We have not yet confirmed the new amounts.

## Frequently asked questions

**P39.** How much does the HQA visa cost in total?

**P40.** For one person, expect about €175,000 for the package plus legal, government and document costs. A family adds €2,500 to €5,000 per person in provider fees, plus their own government fees.

**P41.** Is €175,000 a government fee?

**P42.** No. It is a private price for a package of services, reported by providers, October 2026. Article 90 does not set a minimum investment payment. No government body sets or checks it.

**P43.** Can I pay in installments?

**P44.** Some providers offer staged payments. This is a contract term, not a legal rule. Get the schedule and the refund terms in writing.

**P45.** Do I pay the €175,000 again at renewal?

**P46.** Ask the provider whether the quoted price is paid once, and what a renewal requires. You pay government fees and, usually, legal fees at each renewal.

**P47.** Why don't you list exact government fees?

**P48.** AIMA changed its fees on 1 March 2026. We only publish numbers we can confirm from an official source.
