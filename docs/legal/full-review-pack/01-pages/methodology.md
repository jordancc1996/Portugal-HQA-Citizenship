# Methodology: How We Verify HQA Visa Information

**P1.** How we verify HQA visa information matters as much as the information itself. Investment migration is full of out-of-date claims and sales copy. This page explains where our facts come from, how we label them, who reviews them and how we correct mistakes.

**P2.** Last verified: 20 October 2026

## Where our facts come from

**P3.** We use sources in this order:

**P4.** Portuguese law as published in the Diário da República

**P5.** Official government and agency sources, such as AIMA, IAPMEI, the tax authority and Portuguese consulates

**P6.** Established Portuguese law firms and reputable publications

**P7.** Provider websites, only for facts about their own products, such as prices

**P8.** We never rely on a provider's claim about the law without checking the law itself.

## How we label facts

**P9.** Scroll sideways to see the full table.

**T1.**

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Checked in the official legal text or an official source |
| Current | Stated by a reliable source and not contradicted |
| Reported | Stated by providers or secondary sources, not confirmed officially |
| Not confirmed | We could not verify it, so we say so |

**P10.** Where the law is unclear, we add a "What we don't know yet" box instead of guessing.

## Legal review

**P11.** We are having every page reviewed by an independent Portuguese lawyer. A page shows its review date once its review is complete. The lawyer is not named at their request.

## Independence

**P12.** If you ask us for a consultation, we may introduce you to advisors or providers, but only with your consent.

## Dates and updates

**P13.** Every page shows a "Last verified" date. We change that date only when we have checked the page again. We log law changes and page updates on our status page.

## Corrections

**P14.** If you find an error, email hello@portugalhqacitizenship.com with the page and the source. We check every report and correct confirmed errors.
