# HQA Visa Minimum Stay: How Long You Must Live in Portugal

**P1.** HQA visa minimum stay rules come down to two absence limits: 6 months in a row or 8 months in total. The highly qualified activity permit is a permit to live and work in Portugal. A temporary permit may be cancelled if you spend more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 non-consecutive months, outside Portugal during the permit's total validity, unless the absence is justified. The Golden Visa's 7-day rule does not apply.

## Key facts

**P4.** 6 months in a row. A temporary permit may be cancelled after more than 6 consecutive months outside Portugal during the permit's total validity.

**P5.** 8 months in total. A temporary permit may be cancelled after more than 8 non-consecutive months outside Portugal during the permit's total validity.

**P6.** Justified absences are an exception. The law allows for absences with a valid reason, but the decision is case by case.

**P7.** 7 days is a Golden Visa rule. It does not apply to the highly qualified activity permit.

## What "no minimum stay" claims mean

**P12.** Some providers say their package needs "no minimum stay" or only a few days a year. The usual argument is that time abroad for the business counts as a justified absence.

**P13.** What we know:

**P14.** The law does allow justified absences.

**P15.** It does not list business travel for an incubator project as an automatic justification.

**P16.** Travel for the company, or for other work, does not cancel the permit if you can prove that activity.

**P17.** A longer absence should be justified to AIMA before you leave, or afterwards in exceptional cases.

**P18.** Your permit depends on carrying out a highly qualified activity in Portugal. A role that happens mostly abroad may not meet that condition at renewal.

### What we don't know yet

**P19.** We have not found an official ruling or guidance confirming that HQA package holders can live mainly outside Portugal. If a provider makes this claim, ask for it in writing, with the legal basis, and have your own lawyer review it.

## Tax residence is a separate question

**P22.** Living in Portugal can make you a Portuguese tax resident. Portugal generally treats you as resident if you spend more than 183 days there in a 12-month period, or keep a home there you intend to use as your main residence. Tax residence can bring tax benefits, such as IFICI if you qualify, and also tax duties. Get tax advice before you move.

## Frequently asked questions

**P31.** What is the HQA visa minimum stay?

**P32.** There is no fixed number of days. A temporary permit may be cancelled after more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 non-consecutive months, outside Portugal during the permit's total validity, unless the absence is justified.

**P33.** Can I live in the US and keep an HQA permit?

**P34.** Not if you spend most of the year in the US. The permit is for carrying out a highly qualified activity in Portugal.

**P35.** Does business travel count as a justified absence?

**P36.** It can, but the law does not make it automatic. Keep records of every trip and its purpose, and ask a Portuguese lawyer about your plans.

**P37.** Does time abroad count toward citizenship?

**P38.** Citizenship counts periods of legal residence, which means time with a valid permit. If you lose the permit through long absences, that time stops counting.

**P39.** Do family members have the same presence rules?

**P40.** Family members hold their own residence permits, and the same general absence limits apply to them.
