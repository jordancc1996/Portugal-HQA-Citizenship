## Open questions

From the 21 October 2026 open checks, sections 1, 2 and 6, plus the public checklist file.

Trademark. Should the registers at EUIPO, INPI Portugal and USPTO be searched before a company is registered, in case another mark containing "HQA" conflicts with a company name? The site does not treat "HQA" or "Portugal HQA Citizenship" as its mark.

Company country, and what the site would have to publish:

| Where the company is registered | What the site would have to show |
|---|---|
| Portugal | Name, legal form, registered office address, registration number and registry office, NIPC, email plus a second way to contact, VAT number if any. Sources cited in the open checks: DL 7/2004 art. 10 and Companies Code art. 171. A business-domiciliation address can be used as the registered office. This conflicts with not publishing an address or registration number. |
| Another EU country | Similar. Under the e-commerce directive, that country's law applies: a geographic address, register number and VAT number. |
| United States, for example an LLC | The EU e-commerce directive does not apply directly. DL 7/2004 art. 5(3) may still apply Portuguese law. The cautious option in the open checks is to show the legal name, the state of formation, the registered-agent address and an email. GDPR still applies because the site targets people in the EU (art. 3(2)). An EU representative (art. 27) is probably needed, because collecting leads is the site's core activity. The representative's name and contact must appear in the privacy notice. |

Questions from that section: Does DL 7/2004 art. 5(3) bring a non-EU provider under art. 10? Does a registered-agent or virtual-office address count as a geographic address? A PO box generally does not. Does the art. 27(2) exemption apply? Which country is the better place to register if a home address is not to be published?

Draft adviser checks, not yet on the site. The site would also say it does not check the quality of the advice, outcomes, or the provider's financial health.
