# Portugal HQA vs Golden Visa: Cost, Stay and Citizenship

Portugal HQA vs Golden Visa comes down to one trade-off: money against time in Portugal. An HQA investment package costs about €175,000, but you must not be away for more than 6 months in a row or 8 months in total during each permit. The Golden Visa fund route costs €500,000, but you only need to spend 7 days in Portugal in the first year and 14 days after that. Both routes now lead to citizenship after 10 years of legal residence for most applicants.

## Key facts

1. **Different legal basis.** HQA uses articles 61 and 90 of Lei 23/2007. The Golden Visa uses article 90-A.
2. **Only the Golden Visa has a legal minimum.** €500,000 for the fund route. The HQA price is set by private providers.
3. **Very different stay rules.** The Golden Visa needs 7 days in year 1, then 14 days. An HQA permit can be cancelled after more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 months in total, abroad.
4. **Same citizenship wait.** 10 years of legal residence, or 7 for EU and CPLP nationals.

Real estate routes closed to new applicants on 7 October 2023.

The job, research and culture amounts can be 20% lower in low-density areas. The fund and company routes are not reduced.

## Which route fits you

**The HQA package can fit if you:**

- Want to live in Portugal and work on a real project
- Prefer a lower upfront amount
- Accept that the money funds a business that may fail
- May qualify for IFICI tax treatment through your role

**The Golden Visa can fit if you:**

- Want to keep living mainly in another country
- Can invest €500,000 or more
- Want a route set out with a minimum in law
- Want a residence permit as a backup plan, not a move

---

## What both routes have in common

- **Neither guarantees citizenship.** Both need 10 years of legal residence for most people, plus language and other requirements.
- **Both carry investment risk.** A young company can fail. Fund values can fall.
- **Both depend on AIMA.** AIMA's legal deadline to decide is 90 days, extendable by 30.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the HQA visa cheaper than the Golden Visa?**
The main amount is lower: about €175,000 for an HQA package against €500,000 for the Golden Visa fund route. Fees, risk and time in Portugal also differ.

**Which is faster to citizenship?**
Neither. Both need 10 years of legal residence for most applicants, or 7 for EU and CPLP nationals.

**Can I keep living in the US with the HQA visa?**
Not for most of the year. A temporary permit can be cancelled after more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 non-consecutive months, outside Portugal during its validity, unless the absence is justified.

**Can I still buy property for a Golden Visa?**
No. Real estate routes closed to new applicants on 7 October 2023.

**Can I switch from one route to the other later?**
Possibly, but each route has its own conditions. Ask a Portuguese lawyer before you plan on it.
