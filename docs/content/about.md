# About Portugal HQA Citizenship: Independent Information

About Portugal HQA Citizenship: we are an independent information site about Portugal's residence route for highly qualified activity, and the investment packages sold around it. We started this site because most of what we read was out of date, written to sell, or both.

- Explain the HQA route in plain English, with links to the law

## What we are not

- **Not a law firm.** We do not give legal advice.

- **Not a government body.** We are not connected to AIMA, IAPMEI or any Portuguese authority.
