# About Portugal HQA Citizenship: Independent Information

About Portugal HQA Citizenship: we are an independent information site about Portugal's residence route for highly qualified activity, and the investment packages sold around it. We started this site because most of what we read was out of date, written to sell, or both.

## What we do

- Explain the HQA route in plain English, with links to the law
- Show what the investment packages cost and what they do not cover
- Say clearly what is not yet known
- Keep every page dated and legally reviewed

---

## What we are not

- **Not a law firm.** We do not give legal advice.
- **Not a provider.** We do not sell investment packages.
- **Not a government body.** We are not connected to AIMA, IAPMEI or any Portuguese authority.

## How we are independent

We take no referral fees or commissions from any provider, incubator, university, law firm or advisor.

If you request a consultation, we may introduce you to advisors or providers who can help with your situation. We do this only with your consent, and we are not paid for the introduction.
