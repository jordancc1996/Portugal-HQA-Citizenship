# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Portugal HQA Citizenship ("we") collects and uses personal data sent through this website. Last updated: 20 October 2026.

---

## Who we are

Portugal HQA Citizenship is an independent information website at portugalhqacitizenship.com. Contact: hello@portugalhqacitizenship.com.

---

## What we collect

| Form | Data |
|---|---|
| Due diligence checklist | Name, email, your consent choices |
| Consultation request | First and last name, email, phone (optional), LinkedIn profile URL, country of citizenship, planned timing, message (optional), your consent choices |

We do not ask for passport numbers, financial account details or documents.

---

## Why we use it

- To send you the checklist you asked for
- To reply to your consultation request
- To introduce you to advisors or providers, only if you tick the sharing box
- To email you about rule changes, only if you tick the updates box

We use your data on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

---

## Who we share it with

- **Advisors or providers we introduce you to,** only if you consent on the consultation form. They handle your data under their own privacy policies.
- **Service providers who run our website and forms,** such as our form processor (Formcarry) and our hosting provider (Vercel). They process data for us and do not use it for their own purposes.

---

## How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purpose you gave it for, and delete it when you ask us to.

---

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@portugalhqacitizenship.com.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

---

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

---

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
