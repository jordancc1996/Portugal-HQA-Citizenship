# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Portugal HQA Citizenship ("we") collects and uses personal data sent through this website. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

---

## Who we are

Portugal HQA Citizenship operates this website. Contact for privacy questions and requests: privacy@portugalhqacitizenship.com.

---

## What we collect

| Form | Data |
|---|---|
| Due diligence checklist | Name, email, your consent choices |
| Consultation request | First and last name, email, phone (optional), LinkedIn profile URL, country of citizenship, planned timing, message (optional), your consent choices |

We do not ask for passport numbers, financial account details or documents.

---

## Why we use it

- When you request the checklist, we use your name and email to process the request, and the page then shows a link to the checklist. We do not email it.
- To reply to your consultation request
- To introduce you to advisors or providers, only if you tick the sharing box
- To email you about rule changes, only if you tick the updates box

We use your data on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

---

## Who we share it with

- We share a consultation request only with the advisor or provider you asked to be introduced to, and only if you ticked the sharing box. We do not sell your data.
- Formcarry receives the form submissions and Vercel hosts the website.

---

## How long we keep it

Checklist requests: deleted within 30 days, unless you asked for update emails. Consultation requests: kept while we arrange the introduction you asked for, then deleted within 6 months. Update emails: kept until you unsubscribe, and reviewed after 24 months without activity.

---

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email privacy@portugalhqacitizenship.com.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

---

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

---

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
