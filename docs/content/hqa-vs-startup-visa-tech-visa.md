# HQA vs StartUP Visa Portugal: And the Tech Visa

HQA vs Startup Visa Portugal is a common comparison because the routes look alike: both involve a young company and an incubator. The difference is in the legal basis. HQA packages aim for a residence permit for highly qualified activity under article 90. The StartUP Visa uses article 89, which has different family rules. The Tech Visa is a third route, for people hired by a certified company.

## Key facts

1. **Different articles.** HQA uses article 90, the StartUP Visa uses article 89, the Tech Visa uses article 90 through a certified company.
2. **Family rules differ.** Article 90 families skip the 2-year wait. Article 89 families do not.
3. **Different costs.** HQA packages cost about €175,000. The StartUP Visa has no investment minimum.
4. **All lead to the same citizenship rule.** 10 years of legal residence for most nationals.

Article 90 families and the 2-year wait are explained on the [family page](/hqa-visa-family). The package price is explained on the [cost page](/hqa-visa-cost). The shared citizenship rule is explained in the [citizenship guide](/guides/hqa-visa-citizenship-10-year-rule).

## The three routes side by side

| | HQA investment package | StartUP Visa | Tech Visa |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Articles 61 and 90 | Article 89 | Article 90, Portaria 328/2018 |
| Who it is for | Investors taking a highly qualified role in a funded project | Founders with their own startup idea | Skilled workers hired by a certified company |
| Who approves the company | Provider and its partners | An incubator certified by IAPMEI | IAPMEI certifies the employer |
| Investment | About €175,000 (private price) | No minimum; you need funds to support yourself | None |
| Family 2-year wait | Exempt | Not exempt | Exempt |
| Citizenship | 10 years (7 for EU and CPLP nationals) | 10 years (7 for EU and CPLP nationals) | 10 years (7 for EU and CPLP nationals) |

## Which one fits you

**HQA investment package**
- You want a ready-made structure and are willing to pay for it
- You plan to live in Portugal
- You want family members exempt from the 2-year wait

**StartUP Visa**
- You have your own business idea and want to build it
- You can get accepted by a certified incubator
- You accept the 2-year wait for family members

**Tech Visa**
- You have a job offer from a Portuguese company certified for the Tech Visa
- You want residence without investing

**What we don't know yet:** some providers describe their packages in ways that sound like the StartUP Visa. Ask in writing which article your application will use. It changes the family rules.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between the HQA visa and the StartUP Visa?**
The HQA route uses article 90 (highly qualified activity). The StartUP Visa uses article 89 and needs acceptance by an IAPMEI-certified incubator. The family rules differ.

**Is the StartUP Visa cheaper?**
There is no investment minimum, but you build and fund your own startup, and incubators may charge fees.

**What is the Tech Visa?**
A route for skilled workers hired by a company certified for the Tech Visa by IAPMEI. It does not need an investment.

**Which route gets citizenship fastest?**
None. All three need 10 years of legal residence for most nationals.

**Can I switch from the StartUP Visa to an HQA permit later?**
Possibly, if you later meet the highly qualified activity conditions. Ask a Portuguese lawyer, because a change of permit type has its own requirements.
