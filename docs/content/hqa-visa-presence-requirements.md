# HQA Visa Minimum Stay: How Long You Must Live in Portugal

HQA visa minimum stay rules come down to two absence limits: 6 months in a row or 8 months in total. The highly qualified activity permit is a permit to live and work in Portugal. A temporary permit can be cancelled if you spend more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 non-consecutive months, outside Portugal during its validity, unless the absence is justified. The Golden Visa's 7-day rule does not apply.

## Key facts

1. **6 months in a row.** More than 6 consecutive months abroad can cost you the permit.
2. **8 months in total.** More than 8 non-consecutive months abroad during the permit's validity can also cost you the permit.
3. **Justified absences are an exception.** The law allows for absences with a valid reason, but the decision is case by case.
4. **7 days is a Golden Visa rule.** It does not apply to the highly qualified activity permit.

---

## The three things presence affects

People often mix these up. They follow different rules.

| | What it means | What the rule is |
|---|---|---|
| Keeping your permit | Not losing the permit while it is valid | No more than 6 consecutive or 8 non-consecutive months abroad, unless justified |
| Renewing your permit | Getting the next 3-year permit | You must still meet the conditions, including carrying out the activity in Portugal |
| Counting time for citizenship | Building up 10 years of legal residence | Time with a valid permit counts, within a 12-year window |

## What "no minimum stay" claims mean

Some providers say their package needs "no minimum stay" or only a few days a year. The usual argument is that time abroad for the business counts as a justified absence.

What we know:

- The law does allow justified absences.
- It does not list business travel for an incubator project as an automatic justification.
- Travel for the company, or for other work, does not cancel the permit if you can prove that activity.
- A longer absence should be justified to AIMA before you leave, or afterwards in exceptional cases.
- Your permit depends on carrying out a highly qualified activity in Portugal. A role that happens mostly abroad may not meet that condition at renewal.

**What we don't know yet:** we have not found an official ruling or guidance confirming that HQA package holders can live mainly outside Portugal. If a provider makes this claim, ask for it in writing, with the legal basis, and have your own lawyer review it.

## Tax residence is a separate question

Living in Portugal can make you a Portuguese tax resident. Portugal generally treats you as resident if you spend more than 183 days there in a 12-month period, or keep a home there you intend to use as your main residence. Tax residence can bring tax benefits, such as IFICI if you qualify, and also tax duties. Get tax advice before you move.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the HQA visa minimum stay?**
There is no fixed number of days. The rule works the other way: more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 non-consecutive months, outside Portugal during the permit's validity can cost you the permit, unless the absence is justified.

**Can I live in the US and keep an HQA permit?**
Not if you spend most of the year in the US. The permit is for carrying out a highly qualified activity in Portugal.

**Does business travel count as a justified absence?**
It can, but the law does not make it automatic. Keep records of every trip and its purpose, and ask a Portuguese lawyer about your plans.

**Does time abroad count toward citizenship?**
Citizenship counts periods of legal residence, which means time with a valid permit. If you lose the permit through long absences, that time stops counting.

**Do family members have the same presence rules?**
Family members hold their own residence permits, and the same general absence limits apply to them.
